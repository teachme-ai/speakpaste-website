## 🎯 The Launch Playbook: Why These 3 Benefits Win

1. **Instant Typing in Any App (The "Magic" Experience)**: Everyday users hate manual copy-pasting. Being able to just press a physical key (`Fn`) and watch their spoken words print straight into Slack, Notion, or Safari feels like a native operating system superpower.
2. **Zero Battery Drain (The "Invisible" Experience)**: Bloated AI apps heat up laptops, spin the fans, and eat battery life. Mynah's tiny memory footprint (~30MB RAM) means it runs silently in the background without affecting performance or battery.
3. **Setup That Just Works (The "Zero-Frustration" Experience)**: Normal users dread system utilities because permissions popups are confusing and often break. Mynah's self-healing startup resolves mic and accessibility locks automatically.
